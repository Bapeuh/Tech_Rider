# Daily Operations Guide — Lady Liberty

This guide describes the procedures for **opening the installation**, **preparing between sessions**, **maintaining the rooms throughout the day**, **managing headset languages**, **closing the installation**, as well as the **visitor journey and operator script**.

---

# Part I — Daily Operations

## 1. Opening the installation

Complete these steps every morning before opening to the public.

### Server room

- Switch on the computers in the server room.
- On each computer, launch the experience using the **shortcut on the desktop**.
- Check that the experience has launched correctly.

### Experience rooms

- Switch on all headsets in each room.
- Switch on the control tablet.
- On the tablet, connect to the **server assigned to the room**.
- Check that **all headsets in the room appear in the list**.

> **Important:** do not consider the room ready until all expected headsets are visible on the tablet.

### Batteries and charging

- Reconnect the charging stations.
- Place the batteries on the charging stations.

### Preparing the spaces

- Put the bins away in the cupboards.
- Check that all areas are clean and ready to receive visitors.

---

## 2. Between sessions

This procedure begins as soon as a session ends and must be completed before the next group arrives.

### Visitor exit and equipment collection

- Collect the players’ headsets.
- Remove the **facial interface foam** from each headset.
- Place the used facial interfaces in the designated container.
- Hang the headsets on their hooks.
- Escort the players to the exit.

### Preparing the headsets

- Replace the battery in each headset.
- Fit a **clean facial interface** to each headset.
- Wake the headsets from standby.

### Checking the tablet

- Check that **all headsets appear on the tablet**.
- If a headset does not appear, **restart the experience directly in the headset**.
- Wait until all headsets appear as connected.

When all headsets are connected:

- tap the **cross**;
- select **“Quit server and players”**;
- wait to return to the list;
- check that **all headsets reappear correctly in the list**.

> **Important:** the room is ready for a new session only when all headsets are once again present in the list.

### Cleaning

- Clean the used facial interfaces.
- Clean the **inner and outer headset lenses**.
- Store the clean facial interfaces so that they are available for the following rotations.

---

## 3. Preparing the room during the day

Complete these tasks regularly whenever time is available between sessions.

The objective is to keep all equipment required for upcoming sessions ready and to prevent equipment awaiting cleaning or charging from accumulating.

### Batteries

- Regularly check battery charge levels.
- Remove fully charged batteries from the charging stations and store them in the designated area.
- Immediately charge batteries removed from the headsets.
- Always maintain a stock of **charged batteries ready for use**.

### Facial interfaces

- Clean used facial interfaces regularly to prevent them from accumulating.
- Allow cleaned facial interfaces to dry completely.
- Store clean, dry facial interfaces in the designated area.
- Check that enough clean facial interfaces remain for the upcoming sessions.

### Headsets

Use quieter periods to check the general condition of the headsets:

- clean lenses;
- clean and correctly fitted facial interface;
- charged battery;
- headset correctly hung and stored.

Report any headset problem or damage to the floor manager.

### Storage and cleanliness

- Keep operator stations and storage areas clean and organised.
- Regularly put away equipment that is no longer in use.
- Check the bins and empty them when necessary.
- Ensure that circulation areas remain clear.

### Preparing for the next session

Before the next group arrives, check that:

- all required headsets are ready;
- every headset has a charged battery;
- all facial interfaces are clean;
- all headsets appear correctly on the tablet;
- the room is tidy and ready to receive visitors.

> **General principle: batteries → facial interfaces → headsets → storage.**

---

## 4. Managing headset languages

Each room has a **default language configuration**.

| Headsets | Language |
|---|---|
| **1 to 6** | 🇫🇷 French |
| **7 and 8** | 🇬🇧 English |
| **9** | 🇨🇳 Chinese |
| **10** | 🇪🇸 Spanish |

This configuration must be **maintained as the default in every room**.

### Adapting to visitor groups

If a group requires more headsets in a particular language:

- the **reception** or **floating operator** identifies the requirement as early as possible;
- they inform the operator of the relevant room in advance, specifying **the language and number of visitors concerned**;
- the room operator prepares the required headsets in the correct language **before the group arrives**;
- after the group has completed the experience, the headsets are returned to their **default language configuration**.

> **Objective:** anticipate language changes so that the start of a session is not delayed.

---

## 5. Closing the installation

Complete these steps after the final group has left.

### Headsets

- Switch off all headsets.
- Remove the used facial interfaces.
- Clean the masks/headsets.
- Clean the used facial interfaces.
- Store the headsets.

### Batteries and charging

- Unplug the charging stations.

### Server room

- Shut down the computers in the server room.

### Spaces

- Take out the bins and place them in the **main corridor**.
- Check that **all visitor lockers are open**.
- Check that nothing has been left behind in the rooms.

### Final check before leaving the installation

> **Headsets switched off — charging stations unplugged — computers shut down — bins taken out — lockers open — rooms tidy.**

# Part II — Visitor Journey and Operator Script

## 1. Lockers

### Operator script

> Hello and welcome! Please place your belongings in the lockers, then join me in this room.

**The operator points towards the preshow room.**

---

## 2. Preshow

### Operator script

> Welcome to *A Statue for Liberty*.
>
> In a few moments, you will discover in virtual reality the key stages in the creation of the Statue of Liberty and meet the people who took part in its history.
>
> You will be able to move freely and interact with certain elements of the experience.

**The operator then forms groups of 10 people**, grouping visitors by language whenever possible.

> While you wait, please speak quietly so that you do not disturb the other visitors already inside the experience.

---

## 3. Corridor

This area is mainly used for **transition and waiting**.

The operator:

- answers any questions from visitors;
- keeps the group together;
- then accompanies the group to the operator responsible for distributing the headsets.

---

## 4. Room — Equipment and technical briefing

### Distributing the headsets

> Hello, I am going to give you your headsets. Please wait a few moments before putting them on.

**Distribute the headsets to the entire group.**

### Safety instructions

> Before we begin, here are a few safety instructions.
>
> The walls and boundaries you see in the experience generally correspond to real walls, so please be careful not to try to walk through them.
>
> The rest of the environment is virtual: **do not sit or lean on any part of the virtual set.**

### Moving through the experience

> This is a walk-through experience: feel free to move around and approach the groups of characters.
>
> You can also **follow the footprints and yellow spheres** to discover additional content.

### Fitting the headset

> To put on your headset, begin by loosening it using this dial.
>
> Place the front of the headset in front of your eyes. You will see **a blue dot: look directly at it to calibrate your view.**
>
> Then lower the rear of the headset behind your head and tighten the dial.
>
> Finally, adjust the top strap so that the headset is held securely in place.
>
> I will now come around to check the fit and help you adjust it.

### Assistance during the experience

> If you encounter a problem during the experience, **simply raise your hand** and an operator will come to assist you.

### Starting the experience

> In a few moments, the experience will begin and **you will be able to see one another**.
